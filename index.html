01 / THE PREMISE

An anomaly. An impossible god. The end of reality.

Cassandra should not exist, and reality has been covering for her all her life. Objects change. Memories rewrite. People become someone else. She never notices.

Then a starship crashes into her city. Her best friend survives, but something ancient takes hold of her: Persephone, a god who expects reality to obey.

03 / TWO IMPOSSIBLE WOMEN

I

THE ANOMALY

Cassandra

“Reality has been lying to her and she's none the wiser.”

An exhausted college senior in a black hoodie. She wants her old life back. She does not yet understand that her old life never existed.

×
II

THE GOD

Persephone

“She could end everything. She is simply too curious.”

Ancient, unstable and casually cruel. She wears a familiar face, eats ice cream during catastrophes, and finds Cassandra impossible to ignore.

Neither can let the other go and That is the problem.
